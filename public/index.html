How the radar verifies

THE GATE
STEP 01

Read the claim

We take the project's own page at its word — and quote it as a claim, never as a fact. If the page does not publish its mint terms, there is nothing to verify, and the project does not board.

STEP 02

Read the chain

Name, symbol and supply are read directly from the contract on BNB Chain via public RPC. Contract verification status comes from Sourcify. Site counters are never trusted — when they disagree with the chain, the chain wins and the disagreement is shown.

STEP 03

Follow the money

We read what a mint actually costs: mint price, creator fees skimmed per mint, deposits dressed up as "free", refund obligations versus the contract's real balance. That is where fee-farms give themselves away.

STEP 04

Remember everything

Every check is written to Walrus Memory on mainnet, stamped with a Sui checkpoint read through Tatum. Nothing is silently edited: a label change leaves a trail you can ask back in the box below.

Clear — the free lane is proven by the contract itself and every read is clean: verified source, honest terms, numbers that match the chain.
Potential — a real project with a gap: a read the contract will not give up, a counter only the project reports, or a free window whose state is ambiguous.
Fee-Farm Indication — the chain contradicts the "free" claim: hidden per-mint fees, capital locked behind a refund promise, or a free quota that already ran out while the page still advertises it.

The radar re-captures every listing every 2 hours and re-judges it on the fresh evidence: listings move between buckets, new verified projects board, and listings whose contracts die or whose projects end are removed. Every decision is recorded in Walrus Memory with its evidence — the radar's labels are a living record, not a one-time verdict.

Ask the Radar

This ask box answers only from the radar's Walrus Memory — the recorded history of every contract it has ever checked: when it was first seen, what each check found, and whether its label ever changed. If it is not in memory, the radar says it does not know.

Mint RedCeh is a read-only radar: we never mint for you, sell anything, or promise value. The page claim column is the project's own words; the on-chain facts column is read directly from the contract on BNB Chain (public RPC + Sourcify) and re-checked periodically. Bucket labels are one radar's reading of the public evidence on each card — never treat them as a definitive reference, a verdict, or financial advice. The radar re-captures every listing every 2 hours, and a label can change from one capture to the next as the on-chain facts move — what you see is the most recent capture, and things may already have changed on-chain since it was taken. Always do your own research (DYOR) before minting anything.
